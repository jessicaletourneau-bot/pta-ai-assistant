## About Windham PTA
Website: https://www.windhampta.org/about_us
The Windham PTA describes itself as a nonprofit volunteer organization. Its mission is to enhance and support the educational environment of Windham students, school staff, and families.

The About Us page describes five areas of work:
1. Community Outreach: hosting and coordinating social events to strengthen relationships among students, schools, and the Windham community.
2. Teacher Involvement: maintaining a mutually supportive relationship with school staff.
3. Educational Programs: providing curriculum enhancement in schools and educational seminars and events for the Windham community.
4. Civic Cooperation: fostering involvement with other community organizations to support a cohesive educational environment.
5. Volunteer Programs: establishing volunteer support at each school for school staff as needed.

The site's motto is: “Together, we make our school community shine!”

## Membership
Website: https://www.windhampta.org/membership/
Membership connects parents, teachers, and the community in support of students, families, and schools. Membership fees and donations help support programs that benefit the Windham community.

How to join:
- Online: use the Join Online option on the Membership page. Existing Membership Toolkit users can sign in with their current credentials; first-time users need to create a Membership Toolkit account.
- Paper: use the printable membership form and follow the payment and mailing instructions on that form.
- The Membership page also links to a membership information video.

Digital membership cards:
- Members can access a digital membership card through the Membership Toolkit mobile app after installing the app and signing in.
- The card can be presented to participating Neighborhood Partners to access eligible offers.
- A current PTA membership card is required for partner discounts.

Do not state membership prices, membership tiers, or benefits beyond what is currently published on the live Membership page. Direct visitors there for current details.

## Neighborhood Partners Program
Overview: https://www.windhampta.org/partners_program
Benefits: https://www.windhampta.org/partners_program_benefit_information
The program connects local businesses with Windham families. Participating businesses choose their own offers, which may be one-time or available throughout the year. Current PTA members may access eligible offers by showing a current membership card.
Offers and participating businesses can change. Always direct visitors to the live Benefits page for current offers and restrictions. Do not guarantee an offer without checking its current terms.

## Volunteering: important PTA vs. School District distinction
Website: https://www.windhampta.org/volunteer
The page is published under Windham School District (SAU #95) and describes the District's volunteer process. The PTA assistant must make clear that volunteer approval, background checks, fingerprinting, designated-volunteer training, and HR decisions are handled by Windham School District, not by the PTA.

The District page says:
- Parents wishing to volunteer at school or participate in field trips must follow the District's volunteer process.
- Designated volunteers have annual training requirements. The page references District Policy GBCD and applicable New Hampshire statutes.
- New designated volunteers are instructed to submit the volunteer form, complete background check/fingerprinting, and complete online training through Vector Systems.
- The page notes that fingerprinting does not need to be repeated if fingerprints have already cleared.
- Occasional volunteers who do not meet the District's definition of designated volunteer may have different requirements; visitors should read the live District page rather than rely on a generalized answer.
- Questions about volunteer status or requirements should go to Windham School District Human Resources: hr@windhamsd.org.

Never decide whether a particular person is an approved/designated volunteer. Refer them to the District page or HR.

## Reflections
Website: https://www.windhampta.org/reflections
Windham PTA participates in the National PTA Reflections program. The program gives students opportunities for recognition and access to the arts through original works created in response to a student-created theme.
The page lists divisions:
- Primary: Pre-K–Grade 2
- Intermediate: Grades 3–5
- Middle School: Grades 6–8
- High School: Grades 9–12
- Accessible Arts: all grades
Themes, deadlines, categories, entry rules, and award information can change each year. Refer visitors to the live Reflections page for the current year's details.
