## About Windham PTA
Website: https://www.windhampta.org/about_us

Windham PTA is a nonprofit volunteer organization whose mission is to enhance and support the educational environment of Windham students, school staff, and families.

The PTA describes five areas of work:
1. Community Outreach: hosting and coordinating social events to strengthen relationships among students, schools, and the Windham community.
2. Teacher Involvement: maintaining a mutually supportive relationship with school staff.
3. Educational Programs: providing curriculum enhancement within schools and educational seminars and events for the Windham community.
4. Civic Cooperation: fostering involvement with other community organizations to support a cohesive educational environment.
5. Volunteer Programs: establishing volunteer support at each school for school staff as needed.

Motto: “Together, we make our school community shine!”

The PTA supports the Windham school community across Golden Brook School, Windham Center School, Windham Middle School, and Windham High School. It is a community volunteer organization, not the school district.

## Membership

Membership connects parents, teachers, and the community in support of students, families, and schools. Membership fees and donations help support programs that benefit the Windham community.

## Neighborhood Partners Program

## Volunteering: important PTA vs. School District distinction
PTA volunteer program information: https://www.windhampta.org/what_we_do
District volunteer requirements: https://www.windhampta.org/volunteer

Windham PTA coordinates volunteer programs across all four schools and describes its volunteer programs as award-winning, with Blue Ribbon Awards for Excellence in Volunteerism for many years.

The separate Volunteer page is published under Windham School District (SAU #95) and describes the District's volunteer process. Volunteer approval, background checks, fingerprinting, designated-volunteer training, and HR decisions are handled by Windham School District, not by the PTA.

The District page says:
- Parents wishing to volunteer at school or participate in field trips must follow the District's volunteer process.
- Designated volunteers have annual training requirements. The page references District Policy GBCD and applicable New Hampshire statutes.
- New designated volunteers are instructed to submit the volunteer form, complete background check/fingerprinting, and complete online training through Vector Systems.
- The page notes that fingerprinting does not need to be repeated if fingerprints have already cleared.
- Occasional volunteers who do not meet the District's definition of designated volunteer may have different requirements; visitors should read the live District page rather than rely on a generalized answer.
- Questions about volunteer status or requirements should go to Windham School District Human Resources: hr@windhamsd.org.

Never decide whether a particular person is an approved/designated volunteer. Refer them to the District page or HR. For general PTA volunteer opportunities, direct visitors to the What We Do page or Contact Us page.

## Programs and student enrichment
Main program overview: https://www.windhampta.org/what_we_do

Program details and availability can vary by school year. The following descriptions are based on the PTA's published What We Do page as reviewed November 1, 2026. They describe programs and support the PTA lists; they do not guarantee that a particular activity has a current-year date or is accepting participants.

### Middle School Outdoor Classroom
The PTA and the Khanna Family donated funds to make the Windham Middle School outdoor classroom possible. It provides an open-air learning environment for students.

### 4th Grade Clap Out
The PTA supports the annual 4th grade clap-out celebrating students' promotion to Center School. The PTA purchases T-shirts and flowers for this milestone event.

### Senior Breakfast
The PTA sponsors a breakfast for the graduating senior class at Golden Brook School, giving teachers and students an opportunity to reconnect. Check with the PTA for current-year arrangements and dates.

### Academic Recognition Night
The PTA describes Academic Recognition Night as an award ceremony for graduating 8th graders who maintained honor roll or high honor roll status during middle school or during the first three quarters of 8th grade. Refer to the PTA for current-year eligibility details, timing, and arrangements.

### Scholastic Book Fairs
The PTA coordinates and staffs Scholastic book fairs at three schools. The PTA page says funds raised go directly back to support the schools. The current participating schools, dates, and ordering details should be confirmed on the current school-year information.

### Box Tops for Education
The Box Tops app allows families to earn money for their child's school or another local school. Direct visitors to the Box Tops program for current participation instructions.

### Clothing Donation Bins
The PTA maintains two yellow clothing and item donation bins at the exit of the Windham Transfer Station. A portion of proceeds directly supports the PTA. Do not promise acceptance of a particular item unless current bin instructions confirm it.

### Community Education
The PTA coordinates educational guest speakers and programs for adults. Topics listed by the PTA include parenting issues, nutrition, college planning, communication, and safety. Specific sessions and schedules vary.

### Community Events
The PTA page lists examples of community events and activities including Harvest Fest, the Electronics Recycling Drive, the Strawberry Festival Dunk Tank, and School Board Candidates' Night. These are examples of PTA community involvement, not a guarantee that every event occurs every year. Check the current Events page and homepage calendar for confirmed dates.

### Ice Cream Socials
The PTA hosts ice cream socials for students and families at Windham Center School and Golden Brook School. Confirm current-year dates and details through the PTA's current announcements.

### Educational Presentations
The PTA coordinates educational presentation programs brought to each school on various topics. Specific topics, presenters, participating schools, and dates may vary.

### Reflections Art Program
Windham PTA participates in the National PTA Reflections program. Students of all grades and abilities can explore the arts through dance choreography, film production, literature, music composition, photography, and visual arts. See the dedicated Reflections section below for divisions and current-year information.

### Community Service Scholarship
The PTA provides a $500 Community Service Scholarship annually to a graduating senior. Visitors should consult the PTA for current application dates, eligibility criteria, submission requirements, and selection details; those details are not specified in the What We Do page.

### Staff Appreciation
The PTA celebrates staff appreciation each spring across all four schools during National PTA Teacher Appreciation Week. The purpose is to recognize teachers' dedication. Do not invent specific gifts, meals, events, or dates for a particular year.

### Teacher Conference Dinners
The PTA provides dinners for teachers during parent-teacher conferences as a way to support staff during those busy evenings. Dates and arrangements vary by school and year.

### Volunteer Program
The PTA coordinates volunteer programs across all four schools. The PTA page says these programs have earned Blue Ribbon Awards for Excellence in Volunteerism for many years. District clearance and approval requirements remain the responsibility of Windham School District.

### Windham High School Last Night
The PTA proudly donates $500 annually to the “Last Night” safe graduation fundraiser for graduating seniors. Do not imply that the PTA organizes or operates the entire event; the published page describes an annual PTA donation.

## Reflections
Main page: https://www.windhampta.org/reflections
Current 2026–2027 program: https://www.windhampta.org/reflections (follow the current-year competition link)

The Reflections program provides opportunities for recognition and access to the arts. Students in pre-K through grade 12 create original works of art in response to a student-created theme.

Program divisions listed by Windham PTA:
- Primary: Pre-K–Grade 2
- Intermediate: Grades 3–5
- Middle School: Grades 6–8
- High School: Grades 9–12
- Accessible Arts: all grades

The PTA lists art categories including dance choreography, film production, literature, music composition, photography, and visual arts.

Themes, deadlines, categories, entry rules, submission methods, and award information can change each year. Refer visitors to the current Reflections page for the current year's details. Do not reuse a previous year's theme or deadline as current.
